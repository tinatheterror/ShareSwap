📌 What is the Security Deposit for Borrowing?

Borrowing uses a trust-based deposit:

Your trust score lowers the deposit.
Higher trust = smaller deposit.

Each Tier has a base deposit percentage.
Trust score discount is applied on top.

This keeps borrowing safe without punishing good users.

📌 What is the Security Deposit for Renting?

Renting uses cash deposits (not ShareCoins).

The app calculates:

item value (AI estimate)

rental price

deposit amount

You can edit rental price, but deposit is fixed for safety.

📌 Why do I need photos?

Photos help AI:

verify the item

estimate value

place it in the right Tier

calculate fair rates

At least one photo is required.
Clear photos = accurate pricing.

📌 Can I change the Tier or ShareCoin value?

No.
That’s locked to keep the system fair for everyone.

📌 Can I edit rental prices?

Yes.
Rental prices are fully customizable (within platform limits).

📌 What if someone damages my item?

Every transaction has a deposit.
If damage is reported:

The deposit is held

Support reviews both sides

Funds go to the lender if damage is confirmed

📌 What if someone doesn’t return my item?

The deposit covers non-return.
If the deposit isn’t enough, the user’s account is frozen until resolved.

📌 How do I earn ShareCoins?

Lend items

Gift items

Complete swaps

Receive good reviews

Maintain a high trust score

📌 Can I farm ShareCoins with a friend or partner?

No.
We detect repeated transactions between the same two accounts.
Suspicious activity reduces trust scores and ShareCoin earnings.

📌 Is my address shown to other users?

No.
Only approximate neighborhood is shown.
Exact address is shared only when both sides accept a transaction.

📌 Can I list items I want to borrow?

Yes — use the Wishlist to request items you need.

📌 Do I need to meet people in person?

Usually yes — pick-up or drop-off.
You can choose a public meeting spot if you prefer.

📌 What if I want to delete my account?

You can delete it anytime in settings.
Your items, messages, and balance will be removed.
