# Chargebacks and disputes

> DRAFT for review. Not legal advice: counsel must read it before launch. This page states the launch policy; the Terms of Service deliberately do not promise it, so it can change.

A chargeback, also called a dispute, is when a cardholder asks their bank to reverse a payment.

## If you have a problem with a rental

Please contact the other member and use the claim process in the app first. It is usually faster than a chargeback, and you keep your right to contact your card issuer at any time.

## If you are an owner

- At launch, ShareSwap absorbs the amount of a chargeback and the fee our payment processor charges for it.
- We do not take money from your wallet because of a chargeback, and we do not charge you the dispute fee.
- We may ask you in the app for evidence: your handoff confirmation, photos of the item and your messages about the rental. Sending it quickly helps us answer the dispute.

## If you are a borrower

- When a payment you made is disputed, your account is reviewed by a person.
- If the rental is still open, your deposit hold stays in place until that review is done.

## When we may recover a loss

If we find that a loss was caused by fraud or misconduct, for example confirming a handoff that did not happen or listing an item that does not exist, we may recover it from the member responsible and may restrict, suspend or ban their account. A person at ShareSwap decides this and records the reason; it is never automatic. See the Terms of Service for the full wording.
