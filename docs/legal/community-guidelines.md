# ShareSwap Community Guidelines

> DRAFT for review. Not legal advice: have counsel read it before launch. Once approved, this file is the text the app and website show at /community-guidelines.

ShareSwap works because neighbours trust each other with their things. These guidelines are how we keep it that way. They apply to everything you post or send: your profile, listings, photos, messages, reviews and wishlist posts.

## Be respectful

- Treat everyone with courtesy, in messages and in reviews.
- No harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation, and no repeated unwanted contact. If someone asks you to stop, stop.
- No hate. Do not demean or attack people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or health.
- No sexual content, and no sexual messages to other members.
- No graphic violence, and nothing that glorifies harm to people or animals.

## Keep it safe and legal

- Only list things you own or may lend, rent, swap or give away.
- Do not list anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit or recalled, or anything unsafe to hand over.
- Do not list weapons, ammunition, drugs or drug equipment, adult products, or anything else our listing rules prohibit.
- Describe condition honestly, including damage or missing parts, and keep photos to the item itself.

## Be honest

- No scams. Do not ask anyone to pay, or hold a deposit, outside ShareSwap.
- No fake or traded reviews, and no pretending to be someone else.
- Do not use ShareSwap for spam, advertising or selling services.

## Respect privacy

- Do not post other people's personal information (addresses, phone numbers, photos of them) without their consent.
- Meet and hand over items the way the app guides you to.

## Reporting and blocking

- Tap Report on any profile, listing, message, review or wishlist post. Reports are confidential: the person you report is never told who reported them.
- Tap Block on anyone to stop all contact. You will not see each other's listings, messages or profiles.
- We aim to review every report within 24 hours.
- You can also write to support@shareswap.app.

## What happens when these are broken

Content that breaks these guidelines is removed, usually without warning. Depending on how serious it is and whether it is repeated, we may send a warning or ban the account. Hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, and scams lead to an immediate ban. A ban ends your access to ShareSwap, and we may stop the same person signing up again.

If you think we got it wrong, write to support@shareswap.app and we will look again.

Thank you for helping keep ShareSwap a place people are glad to belong to.
