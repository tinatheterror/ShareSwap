## Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

ShareSwap has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. By creating an account you agree that you will not post, send or upload content that breaks our Community Guidelines, including content that is hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, graphically violent, illegal, fraudulent or that exposes other people's private information.

You are responsible for what you post and send. We may, at any time and without notice, remove or hide any content, and warn, suspend or permanently ban any account, that we believe breaks these Terms or the Community Guidelines.

**Filtering.** We use automated checks on text you submit. Some content may be refused when you try to post it.

**Reporting.** Every profile, listing, message, review and wishlist post can be reported from within the app. We review reports and aim to act on them within 24 hours. We do not tell a person who reported them.

**Blocking.** You can block any member at any time. A block stops messages, requests and offers between you, and hides your profiles and listings from each other. If you are in the middle of a request that has not yet reached the handoff, either of you may cancel it with a full refund and no penalty. After the handoff, the request continues to its end, and disputes are decided by ShareSwap.

**Consequences.** Where we find a breach we may remove the content, send a warning, restrict features, or ban the account. Serious or repeated breaches lead to a permanent ban, and we may prevent the same person from creating another account. Content that appears to be illegal may be reported to the authorities.

**Appeals and contact.** If you believe we made a mistake, write to support@shareswap.app. For anything urgent about safety, use the same address and mark it URGENT.

**Acceptance.** You must accept these Terms and the Community Guidelines to create an account. If we change them in a way that matters, we will ask you to accept the new version before you continue.
